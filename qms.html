    Quality Management System

    One DepEd, One QMS — a unified, process-based, risk-aware system aligned with ISO 9001:2015 principles.

    One DepEd, One QMS

    A Single System for Quality Across the Region

    The Quality Management System provides the structured backbone for how QAD and its partner offices plan processes, set objectives, manage risks, evaluate performance, and improve. It follows the Plan–Do–Check–Act logic embedded in the QA cycle and the clauses of ISO 9001:2015.

    ISO 9001:2015

    Orientation framework: Context of the Organization · Leadership · Planning · Support · Operation · Performance Evaluation · Improvement. Certification status: placeholder

    Internal Quality Audit

    Audit → Findings → CAPA → Verification

    The audit loop closes only when corrective actions are verified as effective — then learnings feed the next planning cycle.

    1 · Plan & Conduct

    Schedule audits, assign independent auditors, use controlled checklists.

    2 · Report Findings

    Document observations, nonconformities, and opportunities for improvement.

    3 · CAPA

    Root cause analysis, action plans, owners, and due dates.

    4 · Verify & Close

    Confirm effectiveness, update documents, and standardize improvements.
