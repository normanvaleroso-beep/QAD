    About QAD

    Championing Quality Education in SOCCSKSARGEN

    The Quality Assurance Division of the Department of Education – SOCCSKSARGEN Region XII provides technical assistance, monitoring, evaluation, quality management, and continuous improvement support to ensure that education programs, processes, and services are implemented effectively, efficiently, and consistently in accordance with DepEd policies and standards.

    Serves Regional Office personnel, Schools Division Offices, school heads, teachers, non-teaching personnel, QA personnel, private schools, education program supervisors, Internal Quality Audit Teams, and other stakeholders.

    Quality Assurance Framework

    The QAD Quality Cycle

    A systematic, repeatable cycle that drives compliance, consistency, and continuous improvement across the region.

    Continuous
    Quality
    Improvement
    1. PLANSet quality objectives, standards, schedules, and responsibilities in line with DepEd policies.
    2. IMPLEMENTDeploy programs, processes, and services with competent personnel and proper resources.
    3. MONITORTrack implementation through scheduled monitoring, checklists, and data collection.
    4. EVALUATEAssess results against benchmarks, targets, and stakeholder expectations.
    5. ANALYZEIdentify gaps, root causes, risks, and opportunities using evidence and data.
    6. IMPROVETake corrective, preventive, and improvement actions — then standardize what works.
    7. CONTINUOUS QUALITY IMPROVEMENTFeed learnings back into planning to restart the cycle at a higher level of performance.

    Quality Management System

    One DepEd, One QMS

    The Division champions a unified, process-based, risk-aware quality management system aligned with ISO 9001:2015 principles — building a culture where every process has an owner, every outcome has a measure, and every gap has a response.

    ISO 9001:2015

    QMS orientation follows the ISO 9001:2015 framework of context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement. Certification status: placeholder
